Scope, pricing and handoff

A website quote is only useful when it’s clear what’s included. We’ll work through the details together.

  1. A clear scope and price

    We’ll agree on the pages, features and design work, along with any content or photos you need to supply. New requests can be discussed before they change the budget.

  2. Time to review

    You’ll see the work before it launches and have an opportunity to give feedback. We’ll check the details that matter to your customers on phones as well as larger screens.

  3. A site you can use

    I’ll walk you through updating your WordPress content. We can also plan ongoing maintenance and support, so you know what happens after launch.

Frequently asked questions

Where are you based?

I’m based in Delhi, Ontario, in Norfolk County.

Do you work with businesses in Simcoe, Port Dover and Waterford?

Yes. I work with businesses across Norfolk County, including Simcoe, Delhi, Port Dover, Waterford and Port Rowan. I also work with clients in nearby Tillsonburg and farther afield.

What should I have ready before we talk?

A link to your current site, if you have one, and a rough idea of what you want the new site to do. Existing photos, logos, content and examples you like are useful, but you don’t need a finished brief to contact me.

How much will my website cost?

Custom WordPress websites start at $2,000 + HST. Your quote depends on the pages, design, content, features and any work needed to move an existing site. I’ll spell out what’s included and what I need from you before we start. Support and smaller changes are quoted separately.

Will I be able to update it myself?

Yes. I build in WordPress and plan the editing around your content, so everyday changes don’t depend on me. We’ll cover how to use the site at handoff, and I can help with maintenance and larger changes when you need them.

Will a new website put me first on Google?

No one can promise that. I can build a site that search engines can read, with useful service information, clear page titles and a sensible structure. Your Google Business Profile, reviews, competition and ongoing content also matter. The first goal is a useful site that helps the right people understand and contact your business.
