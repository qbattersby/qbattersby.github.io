The project process

  1. Start with a conversation.

    Tell me about the business, the current website and what needs to change. Designs or a detailed brief are welcome, but you don’t need them to get in touch.

  2. Agree on the scope.

    We’ll work through the pages, design, functionality, content, timing and price. You’ll know what I’m taking care of and what I need from you.

  3. Build, review and hand over.

    You’ll review the work before it goes live. I’ll walk you through the editing setup and we’ll agree on what support makes sense after launch.

Frequently asked questions

Can you do the design as well as the development?

Yes. I can design and build the website, or work from designs your team already has. Tell me where you’re starting and we’ll work out the right approach.

Can I update the website myself?

That’s part of the plan. We’ll identify what you need to change regularly and build the editing setup around it. I’ll show you how to use it before we finish the handoff.

What do you need from me to estimate a project?

A description of your idea or what you need the website to do is enough to start. Share an existing website if you have one, along with any deadline or budget. Designs and a page list are helpful, but not required for the first conversation.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. I work alongside designers and agency teams on custom WordPress builds and front-end development. We’ll agree on responsibilities, communication and handoff. Read about development for agencies.

Can you help with my existing WordPress site?

Yes. Send the site and the problem you’re trying to solve. I can look at fixes, editing issues, performance, accessibility and maintenance before we decide whether a larger rebuild makes sense. See WordPress support.

Can we meet in person?

Yes. I’m based in Delhi, Ontario, and work with clients in Norfolk County, Kitchener–Waterloo and remotely.
