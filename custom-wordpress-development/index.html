Custom website development

Design, editing and launch

A custom build lets us decide how your content and design work together. Here are the things I plan for, so the site is useful to your customers and your team.

Service pages and enquiries

Pages that explain your services, show relevant work and make the next step easy to find.

WordPress editing tools

WordPress blocks and fields for the content you change, with reusable layouts that help keep pages consistent.

Responsive layouts

Layouts, images, navigation and forms checked on smaller screens as well as desktop.

Accessible, readable pages

Attention to headings, contrast, keyboard use and form labels throughout the build. Specific audit requirements belong in the scope.

Search engine basics

Meaningful page content, crawlable links, titles and metadata. For a replacement site, we also plan what happens to existing URLs.

Training and handoff

Editing guidance and an agreed handover of the site and access. Hosting, licensed tools and ongoing support are discussed before you commit.

Working together

The project process

  1. Talk through the job

    We’ll discuss the business, the people using the site, your content, budget and timing. I’ll tell you where I can help.

  2. Agree on the scope

    We’ll settle the page list, design responsibilities, features, editing needs and launch work before development begins.

  3. Build and review

    You’ll review the site before it goes live. We’ll check the agreed layouts, content, forms and editing tools together.

  4. Launch and hand over

    We’ll complete the agreed launch checks and editing guidance, and decide whether you need support afterward.

Frequently asked questions

What does the $2,000 starting price cover?

It’s the starting point for a new website, not a fixed package for every project. The estimate depends on the pages, design work, content, features and integrations involved. We’ll agree on what’s included, what you’re supplying and any ongoing costs before work starts.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

Yes. We’ll identify the content you need to manage and build the editing tools around it. I use WordPress blocks and ACF where they suit the job, and include editing guidance in the handoff. A new feature or a major layout change may still need development.

What will I own, and what has an ongoing cost?

The site is built on WordPress. Before work starts, we’ll agree on ownership and handover, who controls the domain and hosting accounts, and any paid tools or licences. I’ll make those responsibilities clear in the scope so you can make an informed decision.

Do I need a rebuild, or can you improve what I have?

Start by sending me the current site and the problems you’re trying to solve. A focused set of improvements may be enough. My WordPress support service is a good place to start if most of the site still works for you.
