Design fidelity without fragile code

A Figma file is not just a picture to copy. It contains hierarchy, spacing, reusable patterns, content priorities, and design decisions that need to survive real browser sizes and real WordPress content.

I focus on turning those decisions into a site that feels true to the design while still being maintainable. That usually means clean front-end structure, thoughtful responsive behavior, and editable WordPress sections where your team actually needs control.

Figma to WordPress FAQ

  • Can you turn a Figma design into WordPress?

    Yes. I build custom WordPress themes from Figma designs with responsive front-end implementation, flexible editing, and a practical handoff.

  • Do you work with agencies and designers?

    Yes. I often work with agencies, designers, and internal teams that already have approved designs and need reliable WordPress development.

  • Does every Figma section need to be editable?

    No. The best editing setup depends on the content. I usually make repeatable, high-value sections editable and keep purely structural details controlled by the theme.

  • What do you need to estimate a Figma to WordPress build?

    A useful estimate needs the Figma file or preview, page list, responsive expectations, content status, editing requirements, integrations, and launch timeline.
