Using AI in everyday work

AI can help with tasks such as summarising information, sorting incoming requests or preparing a draft for someone to review. We’ll look at where it would be useful, what information it needs and how to check its output.

The solution might combine AI with straightforward automation and an interface your team can use. We’ll agree which steps can run automatically and which need someone’s review, then test the workflow against examples from your business.

From a recurring problem to a working tool

  1. Walk through the work

    Show me how you handle it today, which systems are involved and where time gets lost. We’ll consider whether your existing software can do more before deciding what to build.

  2. Agree on a first version

    We’ll define the users, essential features, integrations and budget. You’ll know what the first version includes and what can wait.

  3. Build, test and refine

    You’ll review the tool as it takes shape. We’ll test the agreed workflow, work through feedback and plan the handoff before it becomes part of daily use.

Scope and costs

Custom software is quoted around the work involved. The number of users, integrations, data and complexity all affect the estimate. We’ll agree on the scope and cost before development begins.

We’ll also cover code ownership, access to accounts, hosting, any AI or other third-party usage costs, and who handles maintenance. Ongoing support is arranged separately.

Frequently asked questions

Can I bring you one small problem?

Yes. A recurring task or an awkward part of your process is enough to start the conversation. We can look at a focused tool or improvement before considering a larger system.

Can you connect the software I already use?

Often, yes. It depends on the access and integration options each system provides. Tell me what you use and what you want to happen between them, and I’ll check what’s possible.

Does the solution need AI?

No. Some tasks are better handled by ordinary automation, a clearer interface or a change to an existing tool. I’ll recommend an approach based on the work it needs to do.

How do you approach client data and AI?

Before choosing an AI service, we’ll discuss the information involved, who should have access, and any requirements for storage or sharing. We’ll agree what can be sent to third-party services and where human review is needed.

What should I send for an estimate?

Describe the task or idea, who would use the tool and the systems involved. Include any budget or deadline you have. A description is enough for the first conversation; please leave out passwords and confidential client records.

What’s taking up your team’s time?

Tell me about the task, the problem or the product you have in mind. We can work out whether a custom tool would help and what a useful first version could look like.
